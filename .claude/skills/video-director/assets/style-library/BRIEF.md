# Image brief: animation style library

Use your built-in image generation tool (image_gen.imagegen). Make one image per style below and copy each PNG to the exact file name given, in this folder. The library's pictures are then cropped to 16:9 and saved as 768x432 WebP (quality 72) under the same names. These are example pictures for a library of animation styles, so a person can compare styles side by side. The images show THE SAME simple scene, drawn in a different style each time:

**The scene (no people):** a small wooden desk by a window. On it: an open laptop whose screen shows a simple picture of green hills under a blue sky, a potted plant with broad leaves, an angled desk lamp that is switched on, a mug and a closed notebook. Seen from a three-quarter front angle.

**The creature** (only where a line below asks for it): a small, round, friendly made-up creature about the size of the mug, with a soft round body, two dot eyes, a small smile and stubby arms, sitting on the desk and waving at the laptop. It is not an animal, a person or any existing character.

**People** appear only where a line below describes one. Show them naturally and respectfully: real proportions, nothing exaggerated or made comic about their face or body.

**Rules for every image**
- Landscape, 3:2 (1536x1024). Keep the desk and everything on it in the middle of the frame with space around it, so the picture can be cropped to 16:9 without losing anything.
- The style must be obvious at a glance, even as a small thumbnail. Push the style; don't make a plain photo with a light filter.
- No readable text anywhere: no words, letters or numbers on screens, papers, signs or interface. Where interface text would be, use blank bars. The only exception is an image whose line below says otherwise.
- No logos or brand marks (the laptop lid is plain), no watermarks, no signature.
- No real or famous people. Don't copy any specific film, show, game, character or artist's work; make an original picture in the style described.
- One picture per file: no grids of variations, no borders, unless the line says so.
- Look at each image after you make it. If the style isn't clear, or there is stray text, make it again before moving on.

**The images**

1. `isometric.png` — Isometric illustration: the desk scene drawn at a strict isometric angle (no perspective; parallel lines stay parallel), standing on a small floating square of floor with a slice of wall and the window, like a tiny diorama. Flat vector colors with a simple light side and shadow side on every object, on a plain pale background. Flat illustration, not a 3D render.
2. `line-icons.png` — Line icon style: the desk, laptop, lamp, plant, mug and window drawn only as thin, even, single-weight dark lines with rounded ends, like a matching set of interface icons. No fills, except one flat accent color for the lamp's light. Plain white background. Part of the plant's outline is unfinished, as if the line is still drawing itself on.
3. `shape-morphing.png` — Shape morphing shown as a motion diagram: on a plain light background, a row of four bold flat shapes from left to right shows one shape turning into another in stages: a solid coral circle, then a squashed rounded blob, then a soft rounded triangle, then a finished flat coral desk-lamp shade on its lamp. Faint motion trails connect the stages. Below, a smaller second row: a green droplet turning step by step into a leaf on a small potted plant. Bold flat solid colors, smooth liquid curves, no outlines, no text or numbers.
4. `layered-parallax.png` — 2.5D layered parallax, shown so the layers are unmistakable: the desk scene is split into four separate flat illustrated cards (back card: wall and window with hills; second card: the desk with laptop and mug; third card: the plant and the lamp; front card: a few out-of-focus leaves at the edge), standing upright one behind the other with clear empty space between them, seen from a three-quarter side angle so you can see each card's thin edge, the gaps, and the soft shadow each card casts on the one behind. Flat vector colors on each card, a plain dark background around them.
5. `abstract-3d.png` — Abstract 3D render, much more abstract than a toy model: there is no realistic desk. Smooth matte and glossy materials in soft lilac, pale gray and one bright orange, soft studio light, gentle contact shadows, plain flat lilac-gray background, lots of empty space. 6. `soft-3d-icons.png` — Soft 3D icons: the laptop, the lamp, the plant and the mug each made as a separate chubby, rounded 3D icon, like soft matte clay or plastic toys, in a loose row floating above a plain flat pale background with a soft shadow under each. 10. `minimal-mono.png` — Minimal monochrome: the desk scene as a very simple graphic in black, white and gray only, with lots of empty space, except the light from the desk lamp, which is one bright accent color (vivid yellow). Clean, quiet and premium.

### 3D and texture

1. `character-3d.png` — Rounded, appealing 3D animated-film look: the creature as a lit 3D character sitting on the desk waving at the laptop, soft lighting, smooth materials, expressive dot eyes, a warm cozy set.
2. `painterly-3d.png` — A 3D animated-film frame of the desk with chunky stylized sculpted forms (a blocky desk, a heavy angular lamp, an oversized mug) and hand-painted textures: visible brushstrokes painted onto the 3D surfaces, painted rim light, dramatic lighting with teal shadows and warm orange highlights. 3. `skeuomorphic.png` — On the desk, instead of a plain laptop, a chunky physical-looking machine: brushed metal and leather texture, a round analog needle meter, real toggle switches and knobs, and a glass screen showing the hills. Realistic textures and shadows.

Keep the icons dark enough to see clearly.
5. `topographic.png` — Drawn as a topographic map on white paper: the desk, the laptop, the lamp and the plant are made only of concentric contour lines that follow their shapes like elevation rings, with a dotted route line from the lamp to the laptop and small triangle markers. 6. `flow-field.png` — Generative flow-field art: the whole frame, edge to edge, is filled with thousands of fine thin colored lines (teal, magenta, gold, white) on a dark navy ground, all flowing in smooth curving currents like wind or water. The desk, the lamp, the laptop and the plant appear only because the lines bend and bunch along their outlines; nothing is drawn as a solid object or painted surface. The lines are the whole picture.
7. `spirograph.png` — Fine overlapping looping lines forming radial spirograph rosettes; the desk, the lamp, the laptop and the plant are built from these loops in a few colors on white.

To add a style: write its line in the same form under its group, make the image with Codex as `references/media.md` describes, look at it, crop it to 16:9 and convert it to a 768x432 WebP as above, add its row and picture link in `references/animation-styles.md`, and rebuild the page: `python3 scripts/build_style_library.py --out assets/style-library/index.html`.
