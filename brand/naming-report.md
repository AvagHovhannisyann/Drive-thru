# Naming report: drive-thru coffee & bakery chain, Armenia

**Creative director's final recommendation. Date: 2026-10-10**

> **Recommendation: CHAMPA (ՃԱՄՓԱ / ЧАМПА)**, the warm everyday Armenian word for "road". It carries the traveller's blessing *Ճամփադ բաց!* ("May your road be open!").
> Registry checks today found no CHAMPA, ՃԱՄՓԱ or ЧАМПА mark in Armenia (any class) or in Georgia (any class, Madrid designations included), and nothing in US classes 30/43. Russia and the WIPO global database could not be checked from here and must be cleared before filing (see section 7).
>
> **Action needed now:** the working name in `brand/brand_config.json` is **BARI**. BARI is blocked in Armenia by a registered class 43 mark, **BARI CAFE & RESTAURANT** (reg. 31920, valid to 30.01.2030). The generated assets in `brand/out/` should be regenerated with CHAMPA.

**Limitations (please read):**
- The workflow's shared **web-search budget was used up** before this final pass. No general web, Google/Yandex Maps, 2GIS or social-media searches were run, and all evidence below comes from official registries and domain registries queried directly.
- **Russia (Rospatent/FIPS)** returned HTTP 403 and **WIPO Global Brand Database / TMview** refused automated access (403/302). Russia and Madrid designations to Armenia are therefore **not verified**.
- The Armenian national database **does not include Madrid international registrations designating Armenia**, or applications not yet published. It does include filings up to at least April 2026 (e.g. appl. 20261045 filed 07.04.2026).

## 4. Finalist 1 (recommended): CHAMPA

| | |
|---|---|
| **Armenian (signage form)** | **ՃԱՄՓԱ** (title case Ճամփա) |
| **Latin** | **CHAMPA** |
| **Russian** | **ЧАМПА** (follows the ճ → ч convention, as in Ճամբարակ → Чамбарак) |
| **Meaning** | Colloquial and folk Armenian for *road, way, journey* (literary form ճանապարհ). It is the root of the blessing **Ճամփադ բաց!** (*Champad bats!*, "may your road be open"), said to anyone setting off. |
| **Western Armenian** | ճամբայ, pronounced roughly "jahm-BAH". Diaspora customers will recognise it. |

**Pronunciation guide**
- Armenian: **chahm-PAH** [tʃʼɑmˈpʰɑ]. ճ is a crisp "ch" without a puff of air, փ is an aspirated "p", and the stress falls on the last syllable, as in every Armenian word.
- English: **CHAHM-pah**. Stress on the first syllable is fine. Steer people away from the French reading "SHAM-pa".
- Russian: **ЧА́М-па** is the natural reading and is acceptable. Crew say «чам-ПА́».
- Crew script: menu board «Բարի գալուստ Ճամփա» → window «Ճամփադ բա՜ց» / "Champad bats, have a good road!"

| Russian | «Чампа»: no meaning, no slang. Faint, harmless echoes of «чемпион», «шампанское», «шампур». | Neutral to positive |

**Brand-personality fit**
- **Persona:** *the friend who sees you off.* Warm, generous and quick, with a little folk wisdom but never kitsch. That is the Armenian answer to Dutch Bros' crew warmth, and it copies neither their "Bros" language nor any Starbucks cue.
- **Ritual:** every handoff ends with *«Ճամփադ բա՜ց»*. It is free, distinctive, and it turns a drive-thru transaction into a blessing.
- **System fit:** the existing symbol (a tuff portal arch with a sun rising over two road bands) already says "road", so it carries over unchanged. The menu already has *Ճամփի տուփ / Road box*.
- **Sub-brands:** Champa Cold (iced line), Champa Morning (breakfast), Ճամփի տուփ / Road Box, a loyalty programme that counts **kilometres** ("10 cups = 100 km"), and exit signage "Ճամփադ բաց · Champad bats".
- **Scale:** suburbs and other cities still work, because a drive-thru is always on the road.
- **Pylon:** 6 letters with no ascenders or descenders in caps. In Armenian, the distinctive Ճ and Փ shapes make a recognisable silhouette. Put ՃԱՄՓԱ on top (Language Law) and CHAMPA below.
- **Premium guard-rails:** the folk register must be balanced by a modern geometric wordmark, tuff/oak/bronze materials and restraint (no ornament or folk pattern overload).

**Risks and mitigations**
1. *Rustic register* → modern wordmark, premium materials, specialty-coffee menu language.
2. *US merch conflict* (CHAMPA reg. 7088085 covers cups/mugs and t-shirts/hats) → in the US, sell merch under a composite "CHAMPA COFFEE + logo" only after US counsel clearance. This does not affect AM/GE/RU.
3. *Russia unverified* → full FIPS search before any RU designation.
4. *Colloquial word* → it is a dictionary word, so acceptable under the Language Law. If in doubt, get an informal opinion from the Language Committee.
5. *Pronunciation drift* (CHAM-pa vs cham-PA, "shampa") → harmless. Put one phonetic line on cups ("chahm-PAH: 'the road' in Armenian").

## 7. Single recommendation

**Adopt CHAMPA (ՃԱՄՓԱ / ЧАМПА) as the chain name, subject to Russian clearance.**

It is the only finalist that does all of the following:
- It is a real, warm Armenian word that every Armenian understands instantly.
- It states the highway-flagship idea and still works in the suburbs.
- It comes with a ready-made service ritual (*Ճամփադ բաց*).
- It is clean in the Armenian and Georgian registers.
- It is free of political or religious baggage.

Hold **ARAGO** as back-up 1 and **ZARTO** as back-up 2. Keep **ROVAN** as a coined reserve if Russian clearance fails for all three. Do **not** use BARI: it is blocked in class 43, and *Բարի լույս* is blocked in class 30 by Grand Candy's ԲԱՐԻԼՈՒՅՍ ([record](https://old.aipa.am/search_mods/tm_database/view_item.php?id=20241647)). Update `brand/brand_config.json` and regenerate `brand/out/`.
