## 2. Decision

**Product.** Specialty-grade but mainstream coffee plus a narrow line of Armenian hand-held breakfast and bakery items, bake-off or assembled, all-electric, with no fryer, grill or cook.

**Format for unit 1 (CHAMPA Compact).**
- A 90–120 m² pavilion with one drive-thru lane, a tablet runner, a walk-up window and a 10–12-car stack. Where the host forecourt can absorb overflow, ≈7 cars on our own lane is acceptable.
- Preferred siting: a leased corner of a fuel-station or interchange forecourt on a year-round Yerevan exit corridor. The partner provides restrooms (under a cleanliness SLA), paving and the road connection.
- Alternative: a compact standalone pad on an urban-edge arterial with an existing compliant access.
- No indoor seating, no EV or convenience retail in unit 1. Seats, terrace and own restrooms only at a proven site (phase 2 or the reference prototype).
- A second order lane only where weekend counts show more than 45 arrivals per hour.

**Positioning.** Affordable premium with an entry tier in every category. "American-style" is dropped from consumer positioning and from the headline of the pitch. It survives as measurable internal service standards (operations plan): ≤45 s at the pickup window at peak, ≤90 s order-to-handoff for drinks, ≤3:30 from order point to exit, ≥96% accuracy.

**Investment logic.** Stage-gated. The challenge showed that even the best configuration loses money at the C6 base of ≈110 transactions/day. So no build capex is committed until Phase 0 field evidence shows a site that can sustain ≥150 tx/day (see the financial and decision chapters).

## 4. Average ticket (ATV)

- **Base ≈1,850 AMD per order incl. VAT; range 1,700–2,050** (ESTIMATE: 1.0–1.05 drinks × 1,400–1,522 average drink + 30–35% food attach × ≈975 average food item).
- 2,400–2,650 is used only as a June–August leisure upside, not as the base.
- Evidence anchors: A2 puts drinks at 1,300–1,500 per order, "not 1,800+"; A4 puts 1,540–1,860 per order including food; Coffee Like ≈1,672 per check.
- The lenses' 2,050 / 2,400 / 2,650 were rejected as above the anchors.
