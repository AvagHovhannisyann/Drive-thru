Brand identity: CHAMPA ՃԱՄՓԱ

The recommended name is CHAMPA (ՃԱՄՓԱ / ЧАМПА), the everyday Armenian word for "road". It carries the traveller's blessing Ճամփադ բաց! ("May your road be open"), which becomes the sign-off at every pickup window. Registry checks on 10 October 2026 found no conflicting mark in Armenia or Georgia; Russia and Madrid designations still need an attorney's clearance before any signage is ordered.

Why this name

The founder asked for an American-style drive-thru. The research does not support leading with "American": the local leader, Coffee House, already runs a drive-thru on the M4 at mass-market prices [A2 §1]. A name derived from Starbucks or Dutch Bros would also invite legal risk. CHAMPA keeps the American format (speed, lanes, order-ahead, crew energy) and gives it an Armenian voice.

Logo and symbol

The symbol is a portal arch in apricot, the shape of a tuff doorway and of a road vanishing into the distance, with a half-sun rising at its base. It is secular (no cross, no khachkar motif), reads at pylon distance, and works as a single-colour stamp on cups and lids. The wordmark is set in Unbounded ExtraBold; the Armenian-script form always leads, as the Law on Language and Law on Advertising require [C5 §3.6].

Colour palette

Basalt teal and apricot carry the brand; tuff rose ties the building to Armenian stone. Apricot is reserved for the symbol, calls to action and lane guidance so it stays loud.

Typography

Tagline and voice

  • Signature line: Ճամփադ բաց։ / "May your road be open." Used on the exit sign, the cup sleeve and spoken at every window.
  • Persona: the friend who sees you off. Warm, quick, a little folk wisdom, never kitsch.
  • Sub-brands: Champa Morning (breakfast), Champa Cold (iced line), Ճամփի տուփ / Road Box (shareable snack box), and a loyalty programme that counts "road kilometres" (10 cups = 100 km = a free drink).
  • Guard-rails: the folk word is balanced by a modern geometric wordmark, tuff, oak and bronze materials, and no ornament overload.

Packaging

Signage, menu boards, digital and uniforms

Every outdoor item puts ՃԱՄՓԱ first and keeps Latin text equal or smaller in area. The approach sign lists services (coffee, WC, EV) because on intercity roads the fuel stations currently own the stop through toilets, parking and dwell time [A3 §4], and families travelling to the lake value restrooms [B3 §3] ASSUMPTION. Menu-board prices shown are the concept prices from Chapter 4.
