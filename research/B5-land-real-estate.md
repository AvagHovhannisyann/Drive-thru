# B5 — Land & roadside real estate for a drive-thru pad

**Scope:** what a 1,500–3,500 m² roadside pad costs to buy or lease along the candidate corridors (prototype plot 3,256 m²). Covers the M-4 Yerevan–Sevan, M-5 Yerevan–Zvartnots/Ejmiatsin, M-1 Yerevan–Ashtarak, M-2 Yerevan–Artashat, and Yerevan arterials and suburbs.
**Date:** 2026-10-10. **FX:** CBA 361.93 AMD/USD.
**Builds on:** B4 §3.6 (12 earlier land listings), C5 §3.4–3.5 (designation change, road zones), C4 §3.8 (land and property tax), C1 §3.5 (utility connections). This file references those sections and does not repeat them.

**Status tags**
- **VERIFIED:** I opened the primary or official source (law text on ARLIS, Cadastre Committee PDF, Yerevan Mayor's decision).
- **REPORTED:** credible secondary source, such as a listing-portal page (asking prices are what sellers claim, not transaction prices) or press.
- **ESTIMATE:** my calculation; the formula is shown.
- **ASSUMPTION:** my judgement; the basis is given.

## 1. Executive summary

1. **No first-line plot on the M-4 interstate itself is listed on any reachable portal.**
   - I scraped about 370 land listings: 347 on senyak.am, 14 on the Ket.am platform (estate.am, bnakaran.com and real-estate.am share one inventory) and 7 on myrealty.am (4 of them outside Yerevan).
   - Along the M-4 they include agricultural and village plots in Ptghni, Arinj, Kotayk village and Geghashen, and resort plots in Tsaghkadzor.
   - None is a commercially designated plot fronting the Yerevan–Sevan carriageway.
   - List.am, the largest classifieds site, returned HTTP 403, as it did for B4.
   - **Conclusion (ASSUMPTION):** first-line M-4 pads are an off-market, broker-and-owner product. Plan the search around brokers, fuel-station owners and community heads, not portals.

2. **First-line or public-designation plots elsewhere ask USD 30–133/m² outside Yerevan, median ≈65 (n = 9, REPORTED).** These are the non-agricultural plots on a first line or with a designation that allows commercial building.
   - M-1 Yerevan exit: Proshyan, 4,000 m², first line, public designation: $132.5/m².
   - M-5 Parakar, Yerevanyan St, first line, 1,724 m²: $125/m².
   - M-2 Yerevan–Artashat highway (Arevabuyr), 1,234 m², public, first line: $64.8/m².
   - Ashtarak (M-1), road to Ejmiatsin, 4,000 m², public, 100 m frontage: $37.5/m².
   - Agricultural plots near the same roads ask **USD 4–64/m²**.
   - Yerevan arterials ask **USD 160–800/m²**, which puts them out of reach for a 3,256 m² pad.

3. **Only one ground lease was found, and it is on the M-1.**
   - **Ashtarak, Yerevan–Gyumri highway 33:** 1,300 m² of "general-purpose" land at an intersection, with all utilities, fenced and lit.
   - Asking rent **AMD 500/m²/month (AMD 650,000/month)**; published 05.08.2026, updated 25.08.2026 (REPORTED).
   - Scaled to the 3,256 m² prototype this is **AMD 1.63M/month**, close to the top of B4's untested placeholder (AMD 1.3–1.8M).
   - Roadside **building** rents in the suburbs run **AMD 3,000–7,300/m²/month** (Parakar, Zovuni, Dzoraghbyur, Abovyan, Ejmiatsin). These are for buildings, not ground leases.

4. **Ground lease by the yield method (ESTIMATE): AMD 0.6M / 1.0M / 1.65M per month for the 3,256 m² pad on the M-4 Yerevan edge.**
   - Formula: land value × yield ÷ 12 (optimistic / base / conservative).
   - Optimistic: USD 60/m² at 10%. Base: USD 100/m² at 10%. Conservative: the Ashtarak market ask, equivalent to about USD 150/m² at 11%. The yield band is 8–12% (§3.3).
   - **B4's placeholder of AMD 1.3–1.8M matches the conservative case, not the base case.**

5. **The Cadastre Committee does not publish land prices.** Its monthly and annual market analyses give transaction counts and areas only.
   - Land sales fell from **25,797 (2024) to 22,802 (2025), −11.6%** (VERIFIED).
   - They rebounded to **2,418 in August 2026, +43.0% y/y**. Kotayk is the largest market at **32.2%** of transactions.
   - Public-construction (commercial) land is a thin market. In August 2026 the marzes recorded **87 such sales averaging ≈1,000 m²** and **17 auction lots averaging ≈280 m²**, with **none in Kotayk**.

6. **Foreign ownership (VERIFIED): an Armenian company is the safe route.**
   - Constitution Art. 60(6) and Land Code Art. 4(3): foreign citizens and stateless persons **cannot own land**, except as the law provides. They can lease and use it.
   - Legal entities, including foreign legal entities and therefore foreign-owned Armenian LLCs, are not restricted.
   - The consolidated Land Code on ARLIS now contains **Art. 4(3.1)**. It lifts the restriction for plots intended for **construction and servicing of public and production objects** (among others). Per ARLIS's note, the amending law HO-13-N of 20.01.2026 takes effect **1 November 2026**. Counsel should confirm this.

7. **State and community land comes by auction (VERIFIED).**
   - Leases of state or community land are capped at **99 years** (agricultural land: 25). The lessee has a **pre-emptive right** to renew and to buy.
   - Leases, building rights and sales of such land go through **competition / public auction**. The starting price is based on the **assessed market value**, and auctions run on an **electronic system** (Land Code Art. 48, 48.1, 67, 68, 76).
   - Land in road strips **cannot be privatised** (Art. 60(11)); it can only be leased.
   - Yerevan's community leases show a real tenor risk. Recent renewals ran **only 5 years** (to 2031) and gave the city a **unilateral termination right on 3 months' notice** for urban-development programmes (VERIFIED, Mayor's decisions).

8. **A plot that needs a change of designation can lose most of its price advantage.**
   - The owner pays the community the **difference in cadastral value** (C5 §3.4). Cadastral values are now "approximated to market" (VERIFIED for Yerevan, at AMD 58,908–90,628/m² in 2026 lease decisions).
   - So a cheap agricultural plot may cost about as much as a public-designation plot once converted.
   - Illustration (ESTIMATE): a gap of AMD 5,000–20,000/m² adds **AMD 16–65M (USD 45–180k)** on 3,256 m².

9. **Buy vs lease (ESTIMATE): lease is the base case.**
   - At a 14% AMD discount rate (C4) over 10 years, buying beats leasing only if land appreciates **≥4.4–5.7%/yr in USD**. That is not evidenced: land transactions fell 11.6% in 2025.
   - Recommended base: a **registered long-term ground lease or building right (15–20 years plus options)**.
   - Buy only if a public-designation first-line plot is offered at **≤USD 60–80/m²**.

## 3. Detailed findings

### 3.1 Method

**Portals scraped with curl and parsed (all accessed 2026-10-10).**
- **senyak.am:** the whole land-for-sale category (**347** listings across 13 pages, with coordinates and descriptions), the land-for-rent category (**2**), commercial for sale (**180**) and commercial for rent (**437**; 336 parsed).
  - I then opened about 50 detail pages to capture the published/updated dates, designation and frontage text.
- **myrealty.am:** I used the site's own search link builder.
  - Land for sale: Kotayk 3, Armavir 1, Ararat 0, Aragatsotn 0, Yerevan 3.
  - Commercially designated land in Yerevan: 0.
  - Commercial for rent in the four marzes: 2. Commercial for sale: 0.
  - The detail pages confirm the site's default currency is USD: listing 161452 shows "8" as the price and states AMD 3,000/m², about $8.3. This resolves B4's currency caveat.
- **Ket.am platform:** estate.am, bnakaran.com and real-estate.am carry the same 14 land listings (same IDs). B4 used 9 of them; the other 5 are added here.
- **geoln.com:** one Proshyan listing, found via search.

**Not reachable:**
- list.am: HTTP 403, as for B4.
- azdarar.am (public notices): connection failed.
- Community websites abovyan.am and parakar.am: HTTP 403. arinj.am: no connection.
- c21.am and dom.am: proxy 502. remax.am: connection reset. myhome.am: empty reply.
- president.am: CAPTCHA. I used the Constitution on ARLIS instead.
- e-auction.am turned out to be a commodity exchange for pledged goods, not a land-auction portal.

**Official sources:**
- Cadastre Committee market analyses: August 2026 monthly, 2025 annual and 2024 annual. The H1-2026 PDF was downloaded but its tables are not machine-readable.
- The cadastral evaluation-zone maps (maps only, no values) and the fee schedule (image-based, not legible).
- The Land Code and the Constitution, read in full text on ARLIS.
- Three Yerevan Mayor decisions of 2026 on community land leases.

**Web searches:** 15 of the 45 budget. Armenian, Russian and English queries on first-line plots, ground leases, fuel-station land leases, community auctions, cadastral values, yields and the foreign-ownership change.

**Price basis:**
- All portal prices are **asking prices** (REPORTED). Transaction discounts in Armenia are not documented here (gap).
- $/m² = asking price ÷ stated land area (ESTIMATE). Where the price includes buildings or a business, I say so and exclude it from the land ranges.

**Corridor assignment:** from the listing's address and description text (for example "Yerevan–Artashat highway 7" or "on Yerevanyan St, before Musaler"). I did not use the coordinates: senyak often places them at the village centroid, and the OSM road extract held for B4 does not cover all corridor sections.

**Excluded:**
- Estate.am d122394 (Tsaghkadzor, $82,000): the header says 10,000 m² but the text says 1,000 m².
- Listings with garbled prices: senyak "$5" for 30 ha, "$40" for 4,300 m², "$1,200,000,000" for a Masis factory.

### 3.5 Legal and structural

**Designation categories and change of designation.**
- Covered in **C5 §3.4**: the Land Code Art. 6 categories, Art. 7 functional designation, and GD 1918-N, about 28 working days plus a council session (realistically 2–4 months).
- Land addition (ESTIMATE): the owner pays the community the **cadastral-value difference** (Art. 7(8), per C5).
- C4 §3.8 and the Yerevan decisions show cadastral values are now **market-approximated**. So conversion may capture most of the agricultural-to-commercial price gap.
- Payment = area × (CV after − CV before). For 3,256 m²: a gap of AMD 5,000/m² costs **AMD 16.3M (≈$45k)**; AMD 10,000 costs AMD 32.6M ($90k); AMD 20,000 costs AMD 65.1M ($180k).
- The real CVs for Kotayk, Armavir, Aragatsotn and Ararat parcels are a **gap**; order extracts per §5.
- **Rule of thumb (ASSUMPTION):** compare plots on a "converted" basis (price + conversion payment + 2–4 months), and prefer plots already titled "public construction" (հասարակական կառուցապատման).

**Foreign ownership (VERIFIED [13][14]).**
- **Constitution Art. 60(6):** foreign citizens and stateless persons do not enjoy the right of land ownership, "except for cases prescribed by law".
- **Land Code Art. 4(2):** the subjects of land relations include RA and **foreign legal entities**, foreign citizens and stateless persons.
- **Art. 4(3):** foreign citizens and stateless persons cannot own land and may only be **land users**. Persons with **special residency status** are excepted.
- **Art. 4(3.1)** (new): the restriction does **not** apply to plots for household and gardening use, individual house construction, **construction and servicing of public and production objects**, or multi-apartment buildings.
  - ARLIS's note: the article "in the part amended by HO-13-N of 20.01.26 enters into force on 1 November 2026", and that law has transitional provisions.
- **Art. 76(4):** state and community land can be leased or granted under a building right to RA citizens, RA and foreign legal entities, and foreign citizens.
- **Practical structure (ASSUMPTION; confirm with counsel):**
  - hold land, leases or building rights through an **Armenian LLC**, which is a legal entity and so unrestricted;
  - foreign individual investors **should not hold title personally**, at least before 1 Nov 2026 and until the HO-13-N transitional rules are read.
- A 2009 proposal to require Government and Defence Ministry permission for foreign-owned companies buying land within 25 km of borders was reported [20]. I found no such provision in the consolidated Land Code text (keyword search). The corridors studied are not border areas anyway, but counsel should confirm.

**State and community land: lease, building right, sale (VERIFIED [13]).**
- **Art. 48(3)–(4):** lease of state or community land is limited to **99 years** (agricultural 25). The lessee has a **pre-emptive right** to renew and to buy on alienation. Leases are granted **by competition through public auction**, with exceptions set by the Government.
- **Art. 48(1)–(2):** with the owner's consent, the lessee may **sublease, pledge**, or contribute the lease right to a company's charter capital.
- **Art. 48.1:**
  - the **building right** (superficies) lets the holder build, own and use buildings for its term, **≤99 years**;
  - it is registrable, freely alienable and pledgeable;
  - buildings revert to the landowner unless the law provides otherwise;
  - on state or community land the **annual fee cannot be below the annual property-tax rate**, and it is granted by competition (Art. 76–80).
- **Art. 76:** within community boundaries the **community head** grants leases and building rights. This must be **by competition** and must conform to land-use schemes and master plans.
- **Art. 66(1)(3):** **direct sale (no auction)** to RA citizens and legal entities of state or community plots already **provided for construction and servicing of public or production buildings**. This is a path from lease to ownership once built (ASSUMPTION on how it works in practice).
- **Art. 67–68:**
  - other sales go **by auction**;
  - the starting price is based on the **assessed market value**, under Government procedure;
  - for community land the head announces the start price on a council decision;
  - auctions are organised by the community head **via an electronic system** (Art. 68, as amended 17.12.2025, HO-505-N).
- **Procedure (C5 §3.4, GD 596-N §42–44):** auction or tender with a draft architectural-planning assignment (APA) attached.
- **Typical outcome:** one documented case (Dalma, 2012) cleared at **+5% over start** [19]. One data point; ASSUMPTION that this is typical.
- **Initiation (ASSUMPTION):** an investor can ask the community head to put a specific plot to auction, but cannot be guaranteed to win it.

**Roadside protection and right-of-way.**
- Covered in **C5 §3.5**: 70 m from the axis outside settlements for interstate roads; 18 m side-visibility setback; first-line plots inside settlements under GD 2404-N; access only with the road manager's agreement; speed-change lanes.
- Land addition (VERIFIED [13] Art. 60(6), (11)): state and community land in settlement common-use areas (streets, roads) and in **strips allocated to public roads, plus land reserved for their future development, cannot be transferred into private ownership**.
- Such strips can only be **leased or used under permit**. Expect any access road, slip lane or shoulder land to stay public (ASSUMPTION).

## 4. Implications for the model

**Lease the pad in the base case and buy only opportunistically.**

1. **Capital.**
   - Buying the base-case M-4 pad costs about **AMD 118M ($326k)**. Add the 2.5% broker fee (REPORTED) and, if it is not already public-designated, a conversion payment of **AMD 16–65M** (illustrative).
   - That is comparable to or larger than C1's whole mid-range build (AMD 183M).
   - Leasing at **AMD 1.0M/month** frees that capital.
2. **Economics (ESTIMATE).**
   - Over 10 years at the 14% AMD discount rate (C4), leasing costs PV **AMD 62.6M**.
   - Buying costs **AMD 86.1M** net of a flat-USD residual value (AMD 117.8M − PV of AMD 117.8M in year 10).
   - Buying wins only if land appreciates **≥5.7%/yr** (≥4.4% if rent is AMD 1.1M).
   - Simplifications: rent escalation, tax effects (rent is deductible, land is not depreciable) and transaction costs are ignored.
   - Volumes, not prices, are the only official trend, and they fell 11.6% in 2025. That does not support a 5%+ appreciation assumption.
3. **Risk.**
   - Community ground leases carry **short tenor and termination clauses** (Yerevan precedent).
   - Prefer a **private landowner**, with a registered lease or **building right** of ≥15–20 years. Alternatively a community plot under a building right won at auction, with Art. 66 direct-purchase eligibility after construction (ASSUMPTION on practice).
4. **When to buy.**
   - The plot is already **public-construction** designated, first line, ≥2,500 m², with access the roads authority will accept (C5 §3.5).
   - The price is **≤$60–80/m²** (M-5, M-1 Ashtarak, M-2, M-4 outer).
   - The investor wants a land asset and holds it through an Armenian LLC.

   - whether the owner would **lease for 15–20 years**, and at what AMD/m²/month;
   - recent **closed** prices.
2. **Cadastre extracts (e-cadastre.am)** for each shortlisted parcel and its two neighbours. Check:
   - owner and rights (ownership, lease, building right);
   - **target and functional designation**;
   - cadastral value;
   - encumbrances (mortgage, servitude, arrest);
   - registered area and boundaries;
   - whether the parcel touches a road strip, which cannot be privatised (Art. 60).
3. **Community heads (Abovyan, Parakar, Masis, Ashtarak).** Ask about:
   - master-plan status (C5 gap);
   - community land on the first line;
   - willingness to auction a lease or building right of ≥15 years;
   - the conversion route and expected payment;
   - local duties (C4).
4. **Roads authority (MTAI / Roads Department).** Ask for a pre-application view on access for the shortlisted plot (C5 §3.5) **before** any deposit.
5. **Fuel-station owners** on the shortlisted stretch (B2 lists about 35 unnamed stations). Ask about pad sub-lease or co-location rent per m².
